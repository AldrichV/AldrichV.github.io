What I keep coming back to

Gears and drivetrains

I've sized gear trains for an e-bike gearbox, two go-karts and a 3D-printed elastic car. The one that taught me the most was the one that didn't work: PLA gears that were fine in CAD softened under load once they were actually meshing.

  • Two-mode e-bike gearbox
  • Hospital delivery go-kart
  • Charity race go-kart

Things that sense and move

Arduino and LabVIEW builds where sensors decide what the motors do: a solar panel that chases the light, a kart with four ways to drive it, a plant that waters itself, and a servo tuned from a bump test all the way to cascade control.

  • Floating solar tracker
  • Servo feedback control
  • Automated plant watering

Energy and sustainability

Electrifying York's campus heating, a hand-crank generator for a community centre, a −20 °C cold room, and a life cycle study of olive oil bottles. I like it when the numbers settle the argument, like a heavy glass bottle costing more carbon than its recyclability saves.

  • York campus heating retrofit
  • Community energy hub
  • Glass vs recycled PET

A bit more about me

I study mechanical engineering at York University's Lassonde School of Engineering. Most of my projects have been team builds, and I've led five of them, from a gearbox to a campus energy plan. I'm happiest when a project ends with something physical to test, even if the test rig is a bathtub and four pool noodles.
