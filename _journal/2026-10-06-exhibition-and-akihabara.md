Day four of the Japan trip, and most of the morning went to the MAPPA Exhibition. It went room by room through Attack on Titan, Jujutsu Kaisen and Chainsaw Man: storyboards, character sheets, pencil sketches, background paintings and a few full sets. Then the afternoon was Akihabara's card shops, and by the end of the night the cards were spread out on the table.
