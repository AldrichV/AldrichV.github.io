Day three of the Japan trip. An early start at Tokyo Station, then a bus out of the city for the whole day: Lake Ashi, the ropeway up to Ōwakudani, eel for lunch, the springs at Oshino Hakkai and, last, the swans on Lake Yamanaka. Low cloud followed us most of the day, and it was a packed train back into Tokyo that night.
