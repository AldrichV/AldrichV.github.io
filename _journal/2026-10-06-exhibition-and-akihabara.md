Day four of the Japan trip, and most of the morning went to the MAPPA Exhibition, the studio's 15th-anniversary expo at the Tokyo International Forum. After a first room of its other shows (Banana Fish, Yuri!!! on Ice), it went room by room through Attack on Titan, Jujutsu Kaisen and Chainsaw Man: storyboards, character sheets, pencil sketches, background paintings and a few full sets. Then the afternoon was Akihabara's card shops, and by the end of the night the cards were spread out on the table.
