Day five of the Japan trip: an evening at teamLab Planets. Room after room of light: a ceiling of lights, a pink room, mirrors, koi on the floor, a room packed with balloons that changed colour, and a dark room full of falling flowers. At the end I coloured in a snake, and it showed up swimming across the wall.
