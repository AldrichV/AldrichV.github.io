Day eight of the Japan trip. We were staying at a ryokan in Hakone, and spent the day down at the nearby lake, exploring and finding shrines.

Afterwards we went back to the area near our ryokan, to a place called Bar You!. That's where I met the wonderful Mr. Hisao, who fed us and served us drinks. When I tried to buy a shirt made from his own artwork, he said "have it as a present", which is what prompted me to take that picture with him.
